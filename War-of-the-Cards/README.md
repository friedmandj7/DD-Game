# War of the Cards

### How to Play

1. The Deal: Shuffle a standard 52-card deck and deal all cards evenly so each player has a 26-card stack, kept face-down.

2. The Turn: Both players simultaneously flip over their top card and place it face-up on the table.

3. Comparing Ranks: Cards rank from Ace (highest) down to 2 (lowest); suits do not matter. The player with the higher card takes both cards and places them at the bottom of their stack.

4. Going to "War": If both players flip a card of the exact same rank, a "war" begins. Each player places three cards face-down on top of their tied card, followed by one new card face-up.

5. Winning the War: The player with the higher new face-up card wins all the cards played during that battle. If the face-up cards tie again, another round of war is repeated.

6. End of Game: Play continues until one player holds the entire deck
